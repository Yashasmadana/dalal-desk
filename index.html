Best week in years.

Nifty is back above 25,000 for the first time since March. Oil cooled, the US vote went India's way, and foreign money came back. The question now is whether it lasts.

81,000+Sensex close, Fri 6 Nov
25,000+Nifty 50, first time since March
₹28L crAdded to investor wealth in a week

Sensex added more than 2,000 points on Thursday and another 1,718 on Friday to settle above 81,000, its biggest weekly gain in years.

The bounce comes at the end of a hard Samvat 2082. Nifty and Sensex logged their worst Samvat-year returns in 15 years. Foreign investors sold a record ₹4.55 trillion, and domestic funds bought a record amount to absorb it.

Muhurat trading opened Samvat 2083 on Sunday. Oil, the Iran war and foreign flows will decide whether this rally has legs.

What moved the market

US vote

Democrats won the midterms. Markets read it as less room for new tariffs and a better shot at an India–US trade deal.

Oil ▼13%

Brent fell toward $99 on US–Iran talk hopes. India imports most of its crude, so cheaper oil helps inflation, the rupee and margins.

FIIs return

Foreign buying came back after months of heavy selling. One good week does not yet make a trend.
